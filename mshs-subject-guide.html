Read this before you look at the scaling labels

The scaling labels in this tool are an estimate. They are not official data.

QTAC does not publish scaling figures for individual Queensland subjects. Unlike New South Wales and Victoria, there is no published table to look up. QTAC also recalculates scaling every year from that year's actual cohort, so no fixed number per subject exists even in principle.

These labels are a reasoned guess based on who typically takes each subject. They are not from QTAC, QCAA or the school, and nobody has verified them. Each one shows its reasoning when you open the subject, so you can judge it yourself rather than trust it.

Do not choose a subject because of its scaling label. Picking a hard subject you dislike to chase scaling usually backfires, because your result in it drops. Choose subjects you are good at and will keep working at, then talk to a Guidance Officer.

How to read this

General subjects have an external exam and count towards an ATAR. Pick these if university is the plan.

Applied subjects are assessed internally and count towards your QCE, but normally only one Applied subject can count in an ATAR calculation.

VET subjects give you a nationally recognised qualification alongside school. They often have fees and are run with an outside training organisation.

Preparatory Year 10 subjects are the lead-in to the matching senior subject. Year 9 subjects are either Core (everyone does them) or Electives.

Scaling labels (dashed outline, Year 11-12 only) read as: Tends up Mid Tends down Capped. "Capped" means an Applied or VET subject, where the real constraint is that normally only one Applied subject counts towards an ATAR at all.

How the pathway map works

Pick a subject and the map shows what it connects to. Forward starts from a Year 9 subject and shows the Year 10 then Year 11-12 options it opens up. Backwards starts from a senior subject and shows what you would need in Year 10 and Year 9.

Links come from the pathways the curriculum guides actually state. Click any box to open that subject's detail.

The three guides are different editions. Year 9 and Year 10 are 2027 guides, the senior guide is 2026-27. A Year 9 student in 2027 reaches Year 11 in 2029, so the senior offering may change before they get there. Treat the far right column as indicative.
